UK money dates: November 2026 to April 2027

Updated 15 November 2026 · Dates checked November 2026

These are the dates between now and April 2027 that change what UK households pay, earn or receive. Each row links to the official page we checked, and to our own guide where we have one.

Sun 1 Nov 2026 to Wed 31 Mar 2027Cold Weather Payment season. £25 is paid automatically for each 7 day spell of very cold weather in your area. How we keep this page right

Every date and figure here was checked against the official page linked in its row in November 2026. Where a figure is not yet set, such as the price cap from January, we give the announcement date instead of a guess. The benefit rates for April 2027 are proposed rates and could change before they take effect. When an announcement lands, we update the row and the date at the top of the page.
