What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect names, addresses or payment details. Like almost every website, our server records standard technical information when a page loads, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you visited and the time. This keeps the site running and secure. It is not used to identify you personally.

If we add a newsletter in future, we will only store the email address you give us, and only after you ask to join. You will be able to unsubscribe from any email in one click.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, for example which guides are read most and roughly where our readers are. This helps us decide what to write next.

Google Analytics sets cookies that store a random, anonymous identifier so it can tell a returning visitor from a new one. These cookies do not store your name, email or any detail that identifies you personally, and your IP address is anonymised.

Because these are not essential cookies, they load only after you agree. When you first visit, a notice appears asking you to Accept or Reject. Nothing is set until you choose, and if you reject, no analytics cookies are used. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser, which brings the notice back.

Affiliate and advertising links

Some links on this site may be affiliate links, meaning we could earn a commission if you buy or sign up through them. Clicking such a link may pass basic information to that company so they can credit the referral. It never costs you more, and it never changes what we recommend. Full detail is on our affiliate disclosure page.

Your rights under UK GDPR

You can ask what personal data we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. Since we hold almost no personal data, most requests are simple to handle. Email support@jagatitlimited.com and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with our response, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK data regulator.

Data retention and security

Server logs are kept only as long as needed to run and protect the site. The site runs over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
