Amazon Associates Rules UK: 14 That Get Accounts Closed

Published 22 August 2026 · Updated 14 November 2026 · 11 min read · Figures checked November 2026

The Amazon Associates rules that close UK accounts are mostly simple ones: three qualifying sales within 180 days, no buying through your own links, no hand typed prices, and links only to people who opted in. Break one and Amazon can keep every penny of unpaid commission. Here are the fourteen rules, checked against Amazon UK's own pages on 14 November 2026.

The short version
  • Most new accounts die before rule one: three qualifying sales within 180 days of applying, or the application can be withdrawn.
  • Your own purchases do not count. Asking friends, relatives, employees or contractors to buy through your links is banned outright.
  • Hand typed prices are not allowed. Prices must come from an Amazon served link or Amazon's API, and your site must not offer price tracking or price alerts.
  • Links in emails, SMS and social media direct messages are allowed only where the recipient opted in. WhatsApp and Telegram deal groups are not named as allowed, and they carry real risk.
  • If Amazon closes your account for a breach, it can stop paying all commission, "whether or not directly related" to the breach. You have 5 business days to appeal.
  • No commission for three years means a maintenance fee of up to £10 and a dormancy closure.

What is the first rule that kills Amazon Associates accounts?

Before any of the content rules matter, there is a threshold. Amazon UK says it reviews your application "once you've driven qualified sales", and it requires at least three within the first 180 days.

If there were not enough qualifying sales in the 180 days after you applied, the application can be withdrawn.

Four things about it catch people out:

  • Your own purchases do not count. Amazon's help page says plainly that personal orders do not qualify. Not through your own links, not through a relative's account, not through a second address.
  • The review only happens after the third sale. Up to then you are provisionally in, not in.
  • Amazon checks every Site you listed. It wants robust original content, with "at least 10 posts" as its own rule of thumb. Websites must be publicly available, recently updated, generally within the last 60 days, and owned by you.
  • Social pages have their own bar. Amazon UK accepts Facebook pages, Instagram, Twitter, YouTube, TikTok and Twitch, and usually wants at least 500 organic followers.

A withdrawn application is not the end. Amazon says you are welcome to reapply once your site is established. But it cannot reinstate an account or Associates ID after rejection, so the old ID does not come back.

This is also why opening accounts in five Amazon marketplaces on day one is poor advice. You will likely miss the threshold in all five at once.

What are the Amazon Associates rules people are closed for?

These are the Amazon affiliate programme rules that matter most in practice. Each one is taken from the UK Operating Agreement or the UK Programme Policies.

#RuleWhat it means in practice
01Disclosure, which is two separate obligationsFirst, the Operating Agreement asks for the statement "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases", shown clearly and prominently. Second, the Policies say you must disclose the relationship "in a clear, concise and unambiguous manner in real time", so near the link, not only in a footer. Influencers are told to use labels such as "#Ad". UK advertising rules require ads to be obviously identifiable as well, separately from Amazon.
02Price and availability displayYou may show prices only where Amazon serves the link containing them, or where the data comes from Amazon's API. Hand typed or scraped prices are not covered. Your site "must not have price tracking and/or price alerting functionality". Where you show prices, Amazon also requires a notice that they are accurate as of the date and time shown and may change.
03No offline, ebook or print linksThe Policies ban promotion "in any offline manner", naming printed material, ebooks and physical mailings. A lead magnet ebook full of Amazon links is a breach.
04Email, SMS and DMs: opted in onlyYou may include links "in emails, SMS and direct messaging from your social media Sites", but only to people who asked to receive them. Your own newsletter is fine. Cold email is not, and you also carry the UK rules on electronic marketing.
05WhatsApp and Telegram deal groupsNeither app is named in the Policies, and neither is on Amazon UK's list of accepted social networks. A public deal channel is not an approved Site, and posting links into groups where people never opted in falls outside the email, SMS and DM allowance. The fact that thousands of these groups exist is not evidence they are allowed.
06No cloakingYou "will not cloak, hide, spoof, or otherwise obscure" your Site's URL, and you may not use a link shortener in a way that makes the Amazon destination unclear. Links that pass a visitor through an intermediate site without a deliberate click do not earn.
07No incentivised clicksNo "consideration, reward, or incentive" for using your links. That covers money, rebates, discounts, points, charity donations and loyalty schemes tied to Amazon visits.
08No self purchases, friends or familyYou may not buy through your own links, directly or indirectly. You may not ask friends, relatives, employees or contractors to do it either. Buying through your links to resell is also banned.
09No trademark biddingNo bidding on "Amazon", "Kindle", any Amazon mark or a misspelling of one. Paid search must send users to your site, not directly to Amazon.
10No Amazon trademark in your domainNo Amazon trademark "in any domain name, subdomain name". Amazon's Trademark Guidelines extend this to names confusingly similar to its marks. A domain like bestamazondeals is a direct breach.
11Original content, defined in April 2026Your Site must contain original content. Third party material must "contain significant commentary, analysis, or transformation". Mass produced comparison pages that restate listings are now a contract problem, not merely an SEO one.
12No Amazon reviews or star ratingsYou "will not display or otherwise use any of our customer reviews or star ratings" unless you obtained them through Amazon's API. Copy pasting Amazon reviews breaches this.
13No double dipping across networksAmazon flags "attempting to claim commissions from both Amazon Associates and another program using the same traffic". It can withhold commission or end your participation.
14No training AI models on programme contentYou may not use Program Content to develop or improve "large language or multimodal models". Data from Amazon's APIs and feeds may not be used to train or fine tune a machine learning model without written approval.

Summarised from Amazon UK's Associates Programme Operating Agreement, last updated 15 October 2025, and Programme Policies, last updated 14 April 2026, both read on 14 November 2026. This is a plain English summary, not legal advice. The agreement itself is the only authority, and Amazon can change it.

What changed in the Amazon affiliate programme rules in 2025 and 2026?

Most Amazon affiliate advice online is recycled from 2021 to 2023 with the year swapped in the title. These changes break the old playbook:

  • 15 October 2025: the current UK Operating Agreement took effect. It speaks of "commission income" throughout, and its disclosure statement is the one in rule 01.
  • Opt in messaging: the current UK Policies allow links in emails, SMS and social media DMs to people who opted in. That is a genuine loosening compared with older advice that banned email outright.
  • 14 April 2026, the 180 day rule: a purchase only qualifies if, "within 180 days of the purchase", the product is shipped, streamed or downloaded, and paid for. Long pre orders and backorders can now earn nothing.
  • 14 April 2026, paid ads: purchases by customers who arrived through "any paid or boosted advertisement linking to Amazon" no longer qualify, whatever keywords were used. Limited exceptions apply. Ads must land on your own site.
  • 14 April 2026, originality: a written definition of original content, requiring commentary, analysis or transformation for additional value.
  • 14 April 2026, onsite content: onsite commission now applies only to Direct Qualifying Purchases of the same ASIN variant. This affects content posted on Amazon itself, not links on your website.
  • 2026, the API: the old Product Advertising API 5.0 is deprecated. Amazon says calls to it now get a 403 error and must move to the Creators API. Amazon's Creators API pages require at least 10 qualifying sales in the past 30 days. A beginner usually cannot get access, so plan around Amazon served links.

A worked example: Sian's baking blog in Cardiff

Sian Morgan runs a baking blog from Cardiff. Her Amazon UK commission was £140 in August, £130 in September and £130 in October 2026.

Amazon UK pays approximately 60 days after the end of each month, once the balance reaches £25 by direct deposit. So August's £140 arrived around the end of October.

On 14 November, September and October are still unpaid. That is £260 sitting in her account.

Now suppose she starts a Telegram channel and posts deals to 3,000 strangers. If Amazon closes her account for it, the agreement lets Amazon cease payment of "any and all commission income" otherwise payable, whether or not it came from Telegram.

That puts the whole £260 at risk, plus whatever November earned. Her blog, which broke no rule, pays for the channel's breach.

The same maths applies to any rule on this page. The bigger your unpaid balance, the more one shortcut can cost.

What happens if Amazon closes your account?

Amazon UK's help page says you must contact Associates within 5 business days of the closure. Use the Contact Us form and choose the subject "Closed Account Appeal".

Curable breaches, such as failing to answer a request for information, can be corrected and appealed.

Closures for a customer trust risk, such as redirecting traffic from an Amazon Site, are only reviewed if you give "detailed evidence" that Amazon's facts were wrong. That is a much higher bar.

The part people do not expect is the money. Amazon may permanently stop paying all commission income "whether or not directly related to such violation". It may also hold accrued commission for a reasonable period after termination to cover returns.

What happens to an inactive account?

Amazon UK's dormancy page is short and clear. If you earn no commission income in the three years before a given month, Amazon may charge an account maintenance fee.

The fee is the lesser of £10 or your unpaid balance, and the account is closed for dormancy when Amazon does this.

Separately, the Policies say that after at least three years of no substantial activity, Amazon can withhold accrued commission with 7 days' written notice. The amount is capped at the gift card payment minimum, which is £25 in the UK.

If you have an old account with a small balance sitting idle, that is what happens to it.

A ten minute compliance check

  1. Is the disclosure statement on every page carrying a link, near the first link and not only in the footer?
  2. Is there link level disclosure, not just a site wide statement?
  3. Are there any hand typed prices on the site? Search your own content for the pound sign and check every result.
  4. Any copied Amazon reviews, star ratings or review counts?
  5. Any Amazon links in a downloadable ebook, lead magnet or printed material?
  6. Any links posted into a WhatsApp or Telegram group, or emailed to people who never opted in?
  7. Does the domain, any subdomain or any social handle contain an Amazon trademark?
  8. Are you running paid or boosted ads that link to Amazon rather than to your own site?
  9. Is every page carrying original analysis rather than restated manufacturer copy?
  10. Is any link shortener hiding that the destination is Amazon?

Ten questions. If any answer is uncomfortable, fix it before it becomes a closure email. The appeal window is five business days, and the money at stake is everything you have not yet been paid.

Frequently asked

What are the main Amazon Associates rules in the UK? Make three qualifying sales within 180 days of applying. Disclose clearly, near your links. Never buy through your own links or ask family to. Show prices only from Amazon served links or its API. Send links only to people who opted in. Keep content original, and never bid on Amazon trademarks.

Can I share Amazon affiliate links on WhatsApp or Telegram? The UK Policies allow links in emails, SMS and social media direct messages to people who opted in. WhatsApp and Telegram are not named, and neither is on Amazon UK's accepted social network list. Posting into deal groups is the risky end of this, so keep links on your approved Sites.

How do I appeal a closed Amazon Associates account? Contact Amazon within 5 business days of the closure, using the Contact Us form with the subject "Closed Account Appeal". Fix the breach first if it is curable. For customer trust closures, Amazon only reviews appeals with detailed evidence that its facts were wrong.

Can I use AI to write Amazon affiliate content? Drafting with AI is not banned in the Policies. Publishing content without commentary, analysis or transformation is a problem, since the April 2026 definition of original content. Separately, you may not use Amazon's programme content to train language models. The test is whether readers learn something the listing does not tell them.

What if I genuinely want the product myself? Buy it normally, without your own link. The Policies ban buying through your Special Links "directly or indirectly", and Amazon says personal orders do not qualify anyway. Using your link gains you nothing and risks the account.

The verdict

Almost nobody is closed for something exotic. They are closed for buying through their own link to hit the three sale threshold, typing prices by hand, or blasting links into a deal group.

Read the fourteen rules once, run the checklist once a quarter, and put the rest of your energy into content.
