The 14 Rules That Get Amazon Affiliate Accounts Closed

22 August 2026 · 9 min read · Checked August 2026
The short version
  • Most new accounts die before rule one: three qualifying sales within 180 days, or the application is withdrawn and the ID cannot be reinstated.
  • Your own purchases do not count. Neither do your family's, and asking them is one of the fastest ways to be banned outright.
  • Manually typing a price into your article is a violation. So is any price-tracking feature on your site.
  • WhatsApp and Telegram deal channels are not compliant, and are one of the most common causes of account closure.
  • If Amazon closes your account, it can withhold every penny of accrued commission, whether or not it relates to the breach.

The rule that kills most accounts first

Before any of the content rules matter, there is a threshold. Three qualifying sales within 180 days of approval, or the application is withdrawn.

Three things about it that catch people out:

  • Your own purchases do not count. Not through your own links, not through a relative's account, not through a second address.
  • Amazon only reviews your application after the third sale. Up to that point you are provisionally in, not in. At review it checks every site you listed: original content (roughly ten or more real posts is the working rule of thumb), publicly accessible, updated within about the last sixty days, and genuinely owned by you.
  • A failed application can be reapplied for. A closed account generally cannot. The Associates ID does not come back.

This is also why you should not open accounts in five marketplaces on day one, which is advice you will see everywhere. You will fail the threshold in all five simultaneously.

The fourteen rules people are actually terminated for

#RuleWhat it means in practice
01Disclosure, which is two separate obligationsFirst, the exact sentence "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases", displayed clearly and prominently. Second, link-level disclosure near each link, such as "(paid link)" or "#ad". It must be in the same medium: a spoken recommendation needs a spoken disclosure. In the UK the CMA and ASA also require the second one independently of Amazon.
02Price and availability displayYou may only show prices if Amazon serves the link containing them, or you pull them from the Creators API. Manually typed or scraped prices are a violation. Your site must not offer price tracking or price alerts.
03No offline, ebook, PDF or print linksNo special links in printed material, mailings, ebooks or spoken solicitation. A lead-magnet PDF full of Amazon links is a breach.
04Email and DMs: opted-in onlyPermitted since October 2025 if the recipient opted in. Your own newsletter is fine. Cold email is not, and you are the legal sender for spam-law purposes.
05SMS, WhatsApp and Telegram deal channelsNot compliant. The policy prohibits special links in SMS and in offline solicitation. Large Amazon deal channels on Telegram and WhatsApp are among the most common ways affiliates lose accounts, and the fact that thousands of them exist is not evidence that they are allowed.
06No cloakingYou may not hide that the destination is Amazon. Pretty-link style redirects are a grey area many people use; the wording targets obscuring that it is Amazon at all.
07No incentivised clicksNo money, rebate, discount, points, charity donation or any other benefit in exchange for using your links. No loyalty schemes.
08No self-purchases, friends or familyYou may not buy through your own links, nor ask or encourage relatives, employees or contractors to. Amazon refuses to disclose how it detects this, which should tell you something.
09No trademark biddingNo bidding on "amazon", "kindle", any Amazon mark or a misspelling of one, on any search engine. Paid search must land on your own site.
10No Amazon trademark in your domain or handleIncludes subdomains, usernames, group names and Associates IDs. A domain like bestamazondeals is a direct breach.
11Original content, tightened April 2026Third-party material must carry "significant commentary, analysis, or transformation". Mass-produced AI comparison pages are now an explicit contract breach, not merely an SEO risk, and closures are already happening.
12No Amazon reviews or star ratings on your siteUnless obtained through the Creators API. Copy-pasting Amazon reviews breaches this and also breaks Google's review-snippet rules.
13No double-dipping across networksCombining or manipulating attribution to claim from Associates and another programme on the same traffic means withheld commission and termination.
14No using programme content to train AI modelsAn explicit ban on using Amazon product content, directly or indirectly, to develop or improve language or machine learning models.

Summarised from Amazon's Associates Programme Operating Agreement and Programme Policies as they stood on 21 August 2026. This is a plain-English summary, not legal advice, and the agreement itself is the only authority. Amazon changes it without individual notice.

What changed in 2025 and 2026

Most Amazon affiliate advice online is recycled from 2021 to 2023 with the year swapped in the title. Five changes break the old playbook:

  • February 2025: Amazon Inspire, the shoppable feed, shut down. Any guide still recommending it is dead content.
  • 15 October 2025: the operating agreement was rewritten. Affiliate links are now explicitly allowed in email, SMS and social DMs where the recipient opted in. Terminology changed from "advertising fees" to "Commission Income". This one is a genuine liberalisation.
  • 14 April 2026: a purchase only qualifies if the product is shipped, streamed or downloaded and paid for within 180 days of the click. Long pre-orders and backordered high-ticket items can now earn nothing.
  • 14 April 2026: the originality clause tightened, and paid or boosted ads linking directly to Amazon expanded as a disqualification ground. Ads must land on your site.
  • 2026: the old Product Advertising API v5 was hard-deprecated in favour of the Creators API, and the access bar rose from three sales in 180 days to ten qualifying sales per rolling 30 days. A beginner cannot get API access. Plan around plugins that display product data without it.

If you are terminated

Contact Amazon within five business days, using the subject line "Closed Account Appeal".

Curable breaches, such as failing to answer an information request, are appealed successfully fairly often. Closures categorised as customer trust risk are only reviewed if you present detailed evidence that Amazon's facts were wrong, which is a much higher bar.

The part people do not expect: Amazon may permanently withhold all accrued commission, whether or not it relates to the violation. If you have three months of unpaid earnings sitting in the account, they are at risk too.

Dormancy, the quiet one

Earn no commission for three years and Amazon charges a maintenance fee of the lesser of ten dollars or your unpaid balance, and closes the account. Separately it may withhold accrued income after three years of no substantial activity, with seven days' notice. If you have an old account with a small balance sitting idle, that is what happens to it.

A ten minute compliance check

  1. Is the exact disclosure sentence on every page carrying a link, above the fold or near the first link?
  2. Is there link-level disclosure, not just a site-wide statement?
  3. Are there any hand-typed prices anywhere on the site? Search your own content for the pound sign and check every result.
  4. Any copied Amazon reviews, star ratings or review counts?
  5. Any Amazon links in a downloadable PDF, lead magnet or printed material?
  6. Any links being posted into a WhatsApp or Telegram group?
  7. Does the domain, any subdomain or any social handle contain an Amazon trademark?
  8. Are you running paid ads that land on Amazon rather than on your own site?
  9. Is every page carrying original analysis rather than restated manufacturer copy?

Nine questions. If any answer is uncomfortable, fix it before it becomes a closure email, because the appeal window is five days and the money at stake is everything you have earned.

Questions people ask

Can I use AI to write affiliate content? Drafting with AI is not banned. Publishing content with no original commentary, analysis or transformation is, as of April 2026. The practical test is whether a reader gets something from your page that they could not get from the product listing.

Is a redirect like /go/product-name allowed? It is a grey area that a lot of established sites use. The rule targets obscuring that the destination is Amazon. A redirect that lands on a clearly labelled Amazon link, on a page that discloses the relationship, is a much weaker case against you than an unlabelled shortener.

What if I genuinely bought the product myself? Buy it normally, without your own link. You cannot earn commission on your own purchase either way, and using the link is the violation.

Do UK rules add anything on top of Amazon's? Yes. The CMA and ASA require clear, upfront disclosure of paid or commissioned content regardless of what Amazon asks for, and enforcement has been increasing. Meeting Amazon's requirement generally meets theirs, but the obligation exists separately. The operating agreement itself was rewritten earlier this year, and what changed for UK Associates in April 2026 lists the clauses that moved.

The verdict

Almost nobody is banned for something exotic. They are banned for buying through their own link to hit the three-sale threshold, typing prices by hand, or running a Telegram deals channel. Read the fourteen rules once, run the checklist once a quarter, and the rest of your energy goes into content.
