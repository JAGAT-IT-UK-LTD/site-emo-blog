Sell Your Old Phone Before Christmas: Trade In vs Resale UK

For a good condition iPhone 15, the best cash quote we found today was £250 from Back Market, against up to £305 in credit from Apple. The cash buyers were within a few pounds of each other, and the manufacturer won only if your phone is close to perfect and you are buying from them anyway. Whatever you choose, sell before Christmas: the phone in your drawer loses value every month.

1. What is my old phone worth today?

We asked each buyer's own website for a quote on 15 November 2026. We used two popular models: an Apple iPhone 15 and a Samsung Galaxy S24, both 128GB and unlocked. Where a site asked about condition, we chose its "Good" grade: light signs of wear, no cracks, everything working.

A few notes on reading the table.

Apple's figure is a ceiling. Its page lists "up to" values and says trade in values vary with condition, year and configuration. Apple's estimator also asks for your serial number before it gives a personal quote. So £305 is the best case, not the good condition price.

O2 Recycle's figures are "up to" too. The service is run by Likewize, and its page says your quote is locked for 7 days. That gives you time to post the phone.

Condition matters most. At musicMagpie, the same iPhone 15 drops from £275 in Excellent condition to £82.50 in Poor. The Galaxy S24 drops from £225 to £45. A cracked screen can cost you more than the gap between any two buyers.

2. Is manufacturer trade in better than cash?

Sometimes, but read what you are being paid in.

Apple applies the value to a qualifying new device, or adds it to an Apple Gift Card. You must be 18 or over. If you trade in at a store, you need valid photo ID. The final value depends on Apple's check of the phone when it arrives.

Samsung offers two routes on its UK trade in page. You can take an instant discount on a new Galaxy, and for that it accepts phones from various brands. Or you can take Samsung Credit to spend later, and for that only Galaxy smartphones are accepted. Samsung Credit lasts 5 years and can only be spent on Samsung.com. It cannot be given to anyone else.

There are catches in Samsung's small print. If you take the instant discount, you have 7 days after your new phone arrives to send the old one. If the old phone does not match your description, you may have to repay part of the discount. Once you have posted it, you cannot cancel.

Our own arithmetic on the Galaxy S24: Samsung's £211 credit is £13 more than Back Market's £198 cash. If you were buying a new Galaxy from Samsung anyway, take the £13. If you were not, cash is worth more, because you can spend it anywhere.

The same logic applies to the iPhone. Apple's top figure of £305 is £55 more than Back Market's £250, by our arithmetic. But that only holds if your phone earns Apple's best grade and you were buying from Apple.

3. Can I get more by selling on eBay?

Possibly, and selling is now cheaper than it was. eBay's fee page says it is free for UK based private sellers. You pay no final value fee and no regulatory operating fee when an item sells. You get 300 free listings a month.

Fees still apply for optional listing upgrades, for overseas delivery, and once you pass 300 listings in a month.

The cost moved to the buyer. On private seller listings, eBay charges the buyer a Buyer Protection fee, which it includes in the price the buyer sees. eBay's help page sets it at a flat £0.10 per item, plus 7% of the price up to £20, 4% of the part from £20 to £300, and 2% of the part from £300 to £4,000.

By our own arithmetic, on a £280 phone that is £0.10 plus £1.40 plus £10.40, so £11.90 on top for the buyer. It does not come out of your money, but it makes your listing look dearer next to a business seller's.

The real costs of eBay are not fees. They are your time, the postage, and the risk of a buyer claiming the phone was not as described. A trade in site checks the phone and pays. A stranger can open a dispute.

4. Worked example: Tom in Swindon

Tom has an unlocked iPhone 15, 128GB. It has a few light scratches on the back but nothing you would notice at arm's length. He wants a new phone for Christmas, but he has not decided which brand.

He gets four real quotes on 15 November:

  • Back Market: £250 cash.
  • musicMagpie: £247.50 cash.
  • O2 Recycle: up to £244.
  • Apple: up to £305, credit only, and only for a top grade phone.

His phone is not top grade, so Apple's ceiling is not his price. Back Market and musicMagpie are £2.50 apart. Either is a sensible choice.

He then asks whether eBay is worth it. To beat £250 by enough to cover postage and an hour of photographs, listing and messages, he needs a buyer at well above £250. If a similar phone sells for £280, an illustrative figure rather than a market price, he is £30 better off before postage, by our arithmetic. That is a fair hourly rate if it sells first time, and a poor one if the buyer returns it.

The last question is timing. Back Market's own page estimates the same phone at £245 in one month, £235 in three and £221 in six. Those are its forecasts, not promises. On those figures, leaving the phone in a drawer until the summer would cost Tom about £29, by our arithmetic.

Tom takes the £250 cash now. He keeps his choice of new phone open, and the money is in his account before the January bills.

5. How do I wipe my phone safely?

Every buyer in our table asks for the same thing: your accounts removed and the phone reset. musicMagpie asks you to remove your iCloud account before sending, and Back Market says iCloud, Google and other accounts must be disconnected. A phone still locked to you is no use to the next owner.

iPhone, from Apple's own support page:

  1. Back up the phone, and move your data to the new one with Quick Start.
  2. If you have an Apple Watch, unpair it.
  3. Check Parts and Service History for any "Finish Repair" message. Apple says you must finish that repair before you can trade in.
  4. Sign out: Settings, tap your name, then Sign Out.
  5. Erase: Settings, General, Transfer or Reset iPhone, then Erase All Content and Settings.
  6. If you are moving to Android, deregister iMessage. Remove the old phone from your trusted devices.

Samsung Galaxy, from Samsung UK's support pages:

  1. Back up with Smart Switch. Samsung warns a factory reset permanently deletes your data.
  2. Remove your Samsung account and every Google account. Samsung explains that Factory Reset Protection can otherwise ask the next owner for your Google login.
  3. Reset: Settings, General management, Reset, Factory data reset, then Delete all.
  4. Take out the SIM and any memory card. Samsung notes a factory reset does not wipe the SD card.

Samsung's trade in page lists its own conditions too. The phone must power on and hold a charge, have no liquid damage, and be reset with all accounts and locks removed.

6. The honest verdict

Sell before Christmas, not after. Back Market's own forecast takes £29 off a good iPhone 15 by May. A phone in a drawer is money going down.

For cash, the main buyers are close. Back Market and musicMagpie were £2.50 apart on the iPhone 15 and under £7 apart on the Galaxy S24. Pick on speed and trust, not pennies.

Manufacturer trade in is worth less than it looks when your phone is not near perfect, when you are not buying from that brand, or when the value comes as credit you must spend in one shop. Samsung's £211 beats £198 cash only if Samsung.com is where you were going anyway.

eBay can pay more, and selling is now fee free for private sellers. It is worth it for a top condition phone and a seller with time. For everyone else, a quick, checked cash sale is the better deal.

Frequently asked

Is it better to trade in or sell my phone? For cash you can spend anywhere, a resale site is usually as good as trade in. On 15 November, Back Market paid £250 for a good iPhone 15 and musicMagpie £247.50. Apple quoted up to £305, but only as credit and for its best grade. Selling privately on eBay can pay more, with more effort and risk.

How much is my iPhone 15 worth in the UK? It depends on storage and condition. On 15 November 2026, a 128GB unlocked iPhone 15 in good condition was worth £250 at Back Market and £247.50 at musicMagpie. musicMagpie paid £275 for Excellent and £82.50 for Poor. Apple listed up to £305 in credit.

Does eBay charge fees to sell a phone? Not for UK private sellers. eBay's fee page says there is no final value fee or regulatory operating fee, and you get 300 free listings a month. The buyer pays a Buyer Protection fee, which eBay includes in the price shown. Business sellers still pay transaction fees.

What should I do before trading in my phone? Back up, move your data, then remove your accounts and reset. On iPhone, sign out of your Apple Account and use Erase All Content and Settings. On Galaxy, remove your Samsung and Google accounts, then do a factory data reset. Take out the SIM and any memory card.

Is Samsung Credit worth taking? Only if you will spend it on Samsung.com. It lasts 5 years, cannot be transferred, and cannot be used for subscriptions or Marketplace products. On 15 November, a good Galaxy S24 earned £211 in credit against £198 in cash at Back Market.
