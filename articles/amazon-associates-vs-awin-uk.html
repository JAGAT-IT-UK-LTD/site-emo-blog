Amazon Associates UK vs Awin vs Skimlinks: Which Pays a Small Site?

Ruhul Amin · Published 26 August 2026 · Updated 25 November 2026 · 10 min read · Figures checked November 2026

For a small UK site, Amazon Associates UK usually pays first and pays most, because it converts best and its £25 threshold is easy to reach. Awin is the better home for two or three specialist retailers, and it no longer charges a deposit to join. Skimlinks comes last. The commission rate is the least useful number to compare. Threshold, payment lag and conversion decide it.

The short version

  • Awin is now free to join. Its current FAQ says publishers pay no deposit or sign up fee. The old £5 deposit, which this guide used to warn about, is gone.
  • Amazon Associates UK is free too, with a 24 hour cookie. It pays 5% on home, kitchen, furniture and books, 3% on most other things, and as little as 1% on groceries and video games.
  • Amazon gives you 180 days to make three qualifying sales. Miss that and the account is closed, and Amazon will not reinstate the ID.
  • Amazon pays about 60 days after the month ends, from £25. Awin pays on the 1st or 15th once the retailer approves the sale, from £20 if you set that threshold.
  • Skimlinks needs £50 before it pays a UK publisher, and says the normal wait from sale to payment is around 90 days.
  • In the worked example below, Amazon earned more than double an Awin retailer at the same 5% rate, because conversion mattered more than the rate. That is the whole point.

1. Is Awin free to join, and what does each network cost?

Yes. Awin is free to join in November 2026. All three networks now cost nothing up front.

This section gets skipped in most comparisons. It decides whether joining is sensible at your size.

NetworkCost to joinUK payment thresholdTypical cookie
Amazon Associates UKFree£25 by bank transfer or gift certificate, £50 by cheque24 hours
AwinFree, no deposit£20 by BACS if you set it. The default is £5030 days is the industry standard, but each programme sets its own
SkimlinksFree, but it keeps a share of each commission£50Depends on the underlying merchant programme

The Awin line is the big change since we first wrote this guide in August 2026.

Awin used to take a £5 deposit at sign up. It was credited back only when you reached your first payment. A site that joined and never earned £20 lost the £5.

Awin's current FAQ says joining as a publisher is free, with no deposit and no sign up fee. Its requirements page lists no deposit either. An older Awin page from 2019 still describes the £5, so you may see it quoted elsewhere. Go by the FAQ.

Awin also says applications are reviewed automatically, in close to real time, or within 2 business days if it needs more information.

One Awin setting matters on day one. If you never choose a payment threshold, the default is £50. Set it to £20 in your account.

Amazon's cookie lasts 24 hours. Awin's usually lasts 30 days. That single difference matters more than the gap between 3% and 5%, and it does not point the way most people assume.

2. How much does Amazon Associates UK pay per sale?

It depends on the category. Here is the Amazon UK rate card as Amazon published it in November 2026.

CategoryAmazon UK commission
Luxury, fashion, shoes, watches6%
Home, kitchen and dining, home improvement, furniture, books, automotive5%
Beauty, luggage, sports4%
All other categories3%
Appliances, mobile electronics2.5%
Grocery, video games1%

So a £40 kitchen gadget pays £2. A £40 video game pays 40p. We cover the full card in our guide to Amazon affiliate commission rates.

Put that next to an Awin retailer offering 8% and Amazon can look poor. That comparison ignores something. Commission is the last term in the sum, not the first. What you earn is:

clicks x conversion rate x average order value x commission rate

Amazon converts well because the person arriving is usually already logged in, with a card saved and an address on file. A specialist retailer you found on Awin often has to win a new customer from scratch.

A conversion rate that is three times higher will beat a commission rate that is 67% higher, every time.

The 24 hour cookie cuts the other way, and honestly so. Amazon's window closes within a day. A 30 day cookie will catch the person who reads your review on a Tuesday and buys the following Sunday. For considered purchases with a long gap before the decision, that is a real advantage. Our guide to what an affiliate cookie window means explains how the clock works.

3. Worked example: Priya in Leicester

A home and garden blog, 4,000 sessions a month

Priya runs a small home and garden site. It gets 4,000 sessions a month, and about 3% of visitors click an affiliate link. That is 120 outbound clicks.

The conversion rates and basket sizes below are our assumptions, to show the method. Yours will differ.

Route A, everything to Amazon. Say Amazon converts those clicks at 8%, so 9.6 orders. At an average basket of £32 that is £307.20 of order value.

Her links are mostly home and kitchen items, which pay 5%. She earns £15.36 a month, or £184.32 a year.

Garden items are not named on the rate card, so they fall under all other categories at 3%. If every sale were a garden item she would earn £9.22 a month.

Route B, everything to one Awin garden retailer at 5%. Say the retailer converts the same 120 clicks at 2.5%, so 3 orders. Its baskets are bigger, £48 each, giving £144 of order value. At 5% she earns £7.20 a month, or £86.40 a year.

Route C, Skimlinks on the same traffic. Assume it earns much like Route B, less the share Skimlinks keeps. Skimlinks says it takes a small percentage, and its payments page does not state the figure. Whatever the cut, Route C lands below £7.20 a month.

At the same 5% rate, Amazon out earned the Awin retailer by £97.92 a year on identical traffic. Even at Amazon's 3% rate it would still be ahead. Conversion is a bigger lever than commission.

Now the thresholds. Amazon needs £25, so she crosses it in month two. Awin needs £20, which takes until month three. Skimlinks needs £50, which takes at least seven months on these numbers.

Two honest caveats on that example. Conversion rates vary a great deal by niche, and a finance or travel programme with a flat bounty per sale changes the picture completely.

And Priya's real answer is not one route at all. It is Amazon for the everyday items, and Awin for the two or three specialist retailers her readers buy from.

4. When does the money reach your bank?

Affiliate income is slow in a way that catches people out. The delay is different at each network.

  • Amazon Associates pays about 60 days after the end of the month in which the commission was earned. A sale in January is paid at the end of March. Add the time it takes to cross £25.
  • Awin waits for the advertiser to approve the sale. Awin gives no network average and says timelines vary by advertiser. Where an advertiser uses automatic validation, it applies at 60 days. Awin then pays on the 1st or the 15th of the month.
  • Skimlinks says the normal period from sale to payment is around 90 days. A small site also has to clear the £50 threshold first.

Our guide to affiliate network payment terms sets out the waiting times side by side.

One deadline is more serious than any of this. When you join Amazon Associates you have 180 days to refer three qualifying sales. Miss it and the account is closed. Amazon states it cannot reinstate the account or the Associates ID afterwards.

You can apply again later with an established site, but the old tag is gone.

The practical point: do not apply to Amazon before you have traffic. Applying early costs nothing in money and everything in clock. Build the site, get the visitors, then apply.

Amazon also has a rule for accounts that go quiet. If you earn no commission for 3 years, it may charge a fee of £10, or your unpaid balance if that is less, and close the account.

5. Which affiliate network should a UK beginner join first?

For a UK site under roughly 10,000 sessions a month, this order works.

  1. Amazon Associates, but only once traffic exists. Free, high conversion, a broad catalogue, and a £25 threshold you can reach. The 180 day clock is the reason to wait, not rush.
  2. Awin, once you know which two or three retailers your readers buy from. It is free now, so there is no cost to being approved and earning little. A longer cookie earns its keep on considered purchases. Set your threshold to £20.
  3. Skimlinks, mostly not yet. Its case is convenience on a site with hundreds of old posts full of plain outbound links. On a small, deliberate site, giving away a share of every commission to automate something you can do by hand is a poor trade.

There is nothing to buy in this article. No tool, course or plugin changes any of the numbers above. Anyone selling you one on the promise that it will is selling you the wrong thing.

6. What must you disclose, and what about tax?

Our August version left this out, and it applies whichever network you pick.

Amazon requires a set sentence on your site. It reads: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

The advertising rules require more. Under the CAP Code, affiliate content must be obviously identifiable as advertising. The ASA says both the affiliate and the brand are responsible. For a blog, the simplest route is a clear label such as "Ad", visible before the reader clicks through.

The CMA's guidance for content creators adds that vague tags such as #aff and #affiliate are terms to avoid. Our guide to UK affiliate disclosure rules shows wording that passes.

Affiliate income is taxable. It counts towards the trading allowance, which is £1,000 of gross income in a tax year now and rises to £3,000 from 6 April 2027. Keep records from the first payment, not the first tax return.

Our guide to side hustle tax rules covers where the thresholds sit, and how to declare affiliate income to HMRC walks through the return.

Frequently asked

Is Awin free to join in the UK? Yes. Awin's current FAQ says joining as a publisher is free, with no deposit or sign up fee. It used to take a £5 deposit that was returned with your first payment, and an older Awin page still describes it. Applications are reviewed automatically, or within 2 business days.

How long does the Amazon affiliate cookie last? 24 hours from the click. Within that window you earn on qualifying items the customer orders, not only the item you linked to. If they add an item to their basket in that window, the order can still count if it is completed no later than 89 days after the click.

Can I use Amazon Associates and Awin on the same site? Yes, and most UK sites should. They are not exclusive. Point each link at whichever merchant suits that product, not at one network for everything. Remember each network has its own threshold, so splitting very small earnings across three networks delays every payment.

What is the minimum payout on Awin and Amazon Associates UK? Amazon pays from £25 by bank transfer or gift certificate, and from £50 by cheque. Awin pays UK publishers from £20 by BACS, but only if you set that threshold in your account. Leave it alone and the default is £50. Skimlinks pays UK publishers from £50.

What if my site gets almost no traffic yet? Then wait. Joining Amazon starts a 180 day clock you cannot pause, and three sales need real visitors. Write more, get readers, then apply. Our beginners guide to affiliate marketing covers the traffic sums first, and it is the better place to start.

The honest verdict

Compare networks on threshold, payment lag and conversion, not on the rate card.

Amazon Associates UK is free to join, converts best, and pays about 60 days after month end once you clear £25. Awin is now free as well. Its longer cookies and specialist retailers make it the right home for the shops your readers already use.

Skimlinks keeps a share of everything and needs £50 before it pays, which makes it a poor first network for a small UK site. Platform programmes work on different terms again, as the gap between Twitch Affiliate and Twitch Partner on payout shows.

In the worked example, two programmes paying the same 5% were £97.92 a year apart. If a comparison article ranks networks by commission percentage alone, it has not done the arithmetic.

And if your site has no traffic yet, joining Amazon today only starts a clock you are likely to lose. Wait. For a sense of the timescale, see how long a first affiliate sale takes.
